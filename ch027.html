背景

I didn’t know they were so salty. ‘How salty they are!’ ‘Didn’t you know that?’ ‘No.’ ‘Why? Haven’t you ever cried?’ ‘Yes, but …’ ‘You’ve cried and you never tasted them? How strange! Will you let me put my hand on your breast?’ She didn’t wait for me to answer.
