PART ONE

Chapter 19庄园、贵族与社会流动

背景

estate、title、inheritance 和 marriage 构成贵族权力的语言。西西里的土地租佃链也让佣人、管理者和地主之间的关系很复杂。

英文原文

19 She had undoubtedly chosen some horrible place where I would necessarily find my vocation. Sister Costanza had been very certain when she said: 'I know that we shall meet again.' With my fists clenched to my temples, which were bursting from that I know that we shall meet again , I didn't hear the door open. 'What's the matter? Are you crying, princess? You loved her so much, did you? Well, I did too. I don't cry, because it's not something men do, but inside, well …! She was a great lady! But come now, come along. It's best for you to go. Dark times are ahead for this convent. Just now, a sealed letter from Palermo arrived. Sister Costanza is replacing Mother Leonora! Dark times! Come, get up, let me take your suitcase. I'll carry it for you. Sister Costanza sent me to you because they mustn't watch you leave … What's wrong? Are you trembling? Don't be upset! Weep for her, of course, because it's fitting that the dead be remembered with tears; she loved you like a daughter. But now come, come with me. You'll see that even though she's dead, she hasn't abandoned you.' I clung to Mimmo's arm – before I would never have been able to do that. I no longer felt like one of them. What could they do to me now, even if they were watching me from the half-closed shutters of all those windows in the courtyard? – I gripped his arm tightly, when my eyes fell upon something so grand it made my legs shakier than my earlier fear had: a carriage without horses. Or were the horses under that long tube that gleamed in the sun? Surely the horses must be in there, looking out from those big glass eyes framed in gold. 'It's not a carriage, princess, it's a modern-day deviltry that runs as if there were ten horses pulling it … I'm old-fashioned and I don't care for all these new contraptions. I'm wary of them. I saw one down in the village, and it seemed like a freak of nature to me, a giant cockroach. What do I know! But I swear to God, this one takes your breath away, it's so beautiful. It looks like a cathedral!' Assisted by Mimmo and a tall gentleman – surely an officer – wearing a dark uniform and a shirt so white that the sisters' wimples seemed grey by comparison, I climbed into that cathedral, though I did not let go of Mimmo's hand. 'There you are, sit right here, signorina . If you feel sick or something makes you uncomfortable during the trip, see this? It's a receiver: pick it up and talk into it. That way I can hear you through the glass if need be.' 'Did you hear that, princess? If you hurt yourself – because this thing runs; it's not like a carriage, it runs like the devil – just lift up that tube and tell him.' 'But who is he, Mimmo? An officer?' 'No, a driver is … is like a coachman … I'll say goodbye now, princess. I know we won't see each other again. This carriage-automobile is grand and Mother Leonora is watching over you. But whatever you may need – by God's grace you are able to write – know that I am here. Remember: Mimmo Insanguine, gardener at the convent of the Sisters of the Sorrowful Mother, Sciarascura. Remember that. Farewell, princess.' The coachman drew my hand from Mimmo's, saying gently, 'Forgive me, signorina , but it's getting late.' And he closed the door. Mimmo's hand lost, I clung to the window glass and looked at him: his arm was raised in farewell. I watched him until his large body, sheathed in brown velvet, blended among the tree trunks, merging with the oaks that surrounded the convent's immense lava wall.
19 她无疑选择了某个可怕的地方，在那里我必然会找到我的天职。科斯坦扎修女说"我知道我们必将重逢"时，语气极为笃定。我双拳紧抵太阳穴——那"我知道我们必将重逢"的话让我的头快要炸裂——我未听见门开的声音。 "怎么了？你在哭吗，公主？你那么爱她，是么？嗯，我也是。我不哭，因为那不是男人做的事，但心里头，唉……！她是位了不起的夫人！但来吧，来吧。你最好离开。这修道院将面临黑暗的时日。方才，一封来自巴勒莫的密封信到了。科斯坦扎修女接替了莱奥诺拉院长！黑暗的时日！来，起来，让我替你拿手提箱。我替你拎着。科斯坦扎修女派我来找你，因为她们不能看着你离开……怎么了？你在发抖？别难过！为她哭泣吧，当然，因为以眼泪纪念死者是应当的；她爱你如同女儿。但现在来吧，跟我来。你会看到，即便她死了，她也没有弃你而去。" 我紧紧抓住米莫的手臂——从前我绝做不到这样。我不再觉得自己是她们中的一员了。即便她们从院中那些半掩的百叶窗后看着我，她们又能奈我何？——我紧抓他的手臂，这时我的目光落在一件极其壮观的东西上，它让我的双腿比我先前的恐惧更加颤抖：一辆没有马的马车。抑或马儿就在那在阳光下闪闪发光的长管下面？那些马必定在里面，从那些镶金边的大玻璃眼里向外望着。 "那不是马车，公主，那是现代的妖术，跑起来像有十匹马拉着一样……我是老派人物，不待见这些新玩意儿。我提防它们。我在山下村里见过一辆，在我看来简直是怪物，一只巨型蟑螂。我哪知道呢！但我对上帝发誓，这辆美得令人窒息。它看起来像一座大教堂！" 在米莫和一位高个子绅士——必定是位军官——的搀扶下，他身穿深色制服，衬衫白得让修女的头巾相比之下显得灰暗，我登上那座大教堂，但我没有松开米莫的手。 "坐这儿，小姐。若途中觉得不舒服或有什么让你们不适——看到这个了吗？这是个听筒：拿起来对着它说话。这样如有需要，我隔着玻璃也能听到你。" "你听到了吗，公主？你若伤到自己——因为这玩意儿跑起来不像马车，跑得像魔鬼一样——只管拿起那管子告诉他。" "但他是谁，米莫？是军官吗？" "不，司机是……就像马车夫……我现在要告别了，公主。我知道我们不会再见面了。这汽车马车很气派，莱奥诺拉院长在天上照看着你。但无论你需要什么——感谢上帝你能写字——记住我在这里。记住：米莫·因桑吉内，忧伤圣母修道院的花匠，夏拉斯库拉。记住。再会了，公主。" 那车夫将我的手从米莫手中拉出，温和地说："恕罪，小姐，天色已晚。" 他关上了门。失去米莫的手，我紧贴车窗玻璃望着他：他的手臂举起作别。我一直望着他，直到他那裹在棕色丝绒中的高大身躯融入树干之间，与环绕修道院那巨大熔岩墙的橡树合为一体。
