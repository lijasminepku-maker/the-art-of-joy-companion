PART THREE

Chapter 64法西斯时期与新的思想

背景

1922 年后法西斯逐步建立一党统治。自由主义、社会主义、无政府主义与共产主义并存。

英文原文

64 When the 'voice' pronounces the word bambina , Modesta becomes a little girl and is compelled to run into Joyce's arms. Or when, side by side, they read those rare books made more precious by long, difficult searches and by the danger posed by simply owning them. If Modesta doesn't grasp a term, Joyce sorts out every obstacle with her melodious voice, revealing an unimagined world of renewed words, retold myths, emotions, events and passions radically uprooted from the old culture, and slid under the clear scientific glass of Freudian analysis … Memory, as key to a new vision, now becomes the prime means of enabling a backward journey into the subterranean woods of seemingly forgotten memories. Brought to light, rearranged and cleansed of mould and incrustations, they reveal mosaics of glittering gems for the understanding of one's own life and that of others. Modesta, disillusioned by the old, idealistic order and by the younger but already decrepit positivism, can't help sensing the innovation and authenticity that Joyce has brought to the island, and tries to make it her own in order to survive in a world where the old God is being replaced by false, barbarian idols in the streets, piazzas and parks. Going out or travelling now means merely swallowing the venom of empty phrases, the poison of a false order and pompous heroism; whereas there, motionless in front of Joyce, beside the new gleam of intelligence that emanates from her face, the hours, months and years glide along on the oiled tracks of a journey even more exciting than a physical one. On this journey, Modesta was always alert in watching for the least little hint of a smile or of sorrow on the beloved face. 64 当那「声音」说出「小宝贝」这个词时，莫德斯塔就变成了一个小女孩，不由自主地奔入乔伊斯的怀抱。或者，当她们并肩阅读那些珍稀书籍——那些因漫长艰险的搜寻、因仅仅拥有它们便足以招致危险而愈发珍贵的书籍——时，若莫德斯塔不解某个术语，乔伊斯便以她悦耳的声音化解一切障碍，揭示出一个未曾想象的世界：被更新的词语、被重述的神话、被彻底从旧文化中连根拔起的情感、事件与激情，置于弗洛伊德式分析的澄澈科学玻璃之下……记忆，作为通往新视野的钥匙，此刻成为回溯之旅的首要工具，通往那些貌若遗忘的记忆地下丛林。被揭示、重排，洗去霉斑与积垢之后，它们显露出镶嵌着璀璨宝石的马赛克，借以理解自己的生命，也理解他人的生命。 莫德斯塔，对旧有的唯心主义秩序、对虽更年轻却已腐朽的实证主义都已幻灭，不由自主地感知到乔伊斯带来这座岛屿的创新与真实，并试图将其化为己有，以求在一个旧上帝正被街头、广场和公园中的虚假野蛮偶像取代的世界里生存下去。如今出门或旅行，不过意味着吞咽空洞词句的毒液、虚假秩序与浮夸英雄主义的毒药；而在那里，一动不动地坐在乔伊斯面前，沐浴着她面庞上散发出的崭新智慧光芒，时光——小时、月份、年月——便沿着油润的轨道滑行，展开一段比肉身之旅更令人心潮澎湃的旅程。 在这段旅程中，莫德斯塔始终警觉地守望着，捕捉那挚爱面容上最细微的一丝笑意或忧伤。她的每一个念头、每一个举动、每一个愿望，都沉浸在审视与预判之中……
