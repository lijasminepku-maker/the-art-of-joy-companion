PART ONE

Chapter 13修道院、教育与规训

背景

convent 不只是宗教场所，也是教育、慈善和纪律机构。理解称谓背后的等级，比记住全部宗教词更重要。

英文原文

13 But acting did not turn out to be as easy as I'd thought. For days and days, masses of clouds raced over the convent like the immense wings of crazed birds, and I was afraid. I went to the well, I stared into its depths, but there too, the cloud masses flapped their dark wings against the slippery walls, only to end up being sucked under by the stagnant water at the bottom. I was shivering with cold. Mimmo was certainly always around now, like a sentinel, and this assured me that his concern was ever vigilant. But he did not approach me again. Surely his apprehension had overcome his relish for chatting with someone, which always did him good. He himself had once told me: 'Forgive me, Voscenza , if I don't talk today, princess. It's just that I'm gripped by thoughts that kill my appetite and my desire to talk.' And coward that I am, I couldn't make up my mind to take the leap that would have liberated both him and me. How could I? I didn't even dare think of those lava walls that slid round and round all the way down to the invisible depths. By day I pounded my head and chest, accusing myself of being cowardly. At night the well's eye never left me, staring at me from the dark corners of the cell, keeping me awake and clinging to the sheets for fear of falling in. I could never do it. It was hopeless. If only I'd known how to swim! If only Tuzzu had taken me to see the sea and taught me to swim! He said it was easy even for a silly ninny, a scemuzza , like me. 'First you have to learn to do the dead-man's float: you just lie on the water like you lie on the grass, on your back, confident, and spread your arms and legs. If you're not afraid, then the water holds you up just like the earth does now.' The dark grass parted under the weight of my back and I let myself go, disappearing inside the well, that eye of water circling, sinking to the depths. Arms and legs splayed like Tuzzu taught me, I gazed fixedly at the sky. But now the black walls were closing in on it, choking it, and the sky was a distant, spinning disc. My head was spinning too … A roar … A thunderous noise … The water, deafened by that roar, sucked me under … I panic, I thrash about, but just as I'm sinking, a hand grabs me by the hair. The shock of the pain rips a short, sharp cry from me that sounds like a laugh. And I want to laugh. Yes, I really want to laugh even if I can't. They were right to be afraid of thunder and lightning striking the lava wall and reverberating inside the well. Mimmo was right: the well had called me and now it was keeping me. But in the end, laughter triumphs over everything – if not mine, that of the gods, which explodes in a thunderous, deafening roar as the lava wall, crumbling and thundering, carries me with it and lets me slip away. Oh, the wonderful slime! Down, all the way down, I slip. Not even in my dreams as a child in the chiana, when Tuzzu would throw me down into the hay from the top of the stack, did I ever slide like this. Down the lava wall, slick with rain dissolved in that very slime, polished by centuries of patient, meticulous work, more polished than the waxed floor of the music room, I slid down without even having to summon the courage I had so anxiously sought for so long.
13 然而表演并不如我想象那般容易。日复一日，大团大团的云朵如疯鸟巨翼般掠过修道院上空，我心中恐惧。我走到井边，凝视深处，但那里亦然，云团在滑腻的井壁上扑动黑翼，最终被底部的死水吞没。我冷得发抖。米莫此刻无疑总在附近，如同哨兵，这让我确信他的关切时时警觉。但他不再靠近我。想必他的忧虑已压倒了他与人闲聊的兴致——闲聊向来对他有益。他曾亲口告诉我： "恕罪，阁下，若我今日不言不语，公主。只是我被思绪攫住，它们扼杀了我的食欲和谈话的欲望。" 而我这个懦夫，竟下不了决心纵身一跃——那一跃本可解脱他，也解脱我。我怎能做到？我甚至不敢去想那些熔岩壁，它们一圈圈旋转着向下通往不可见的深渊。白日里我捶打自己的头和胸，责备自己胆怯。夜里那口井的眼睛从不离开我，从静室的黑暗角落凝视着我，让我无法入眠，紧抓着床单唯恐坠入其中。我永不能做到。一切都无望了。要是我会游泳该多好！要是图祖曾带我去看海，教我游泳该多好！他说即便是我这样愚蠢的小笨蛋也很容易学会。 "首先你得学会浮尸漂：你只需躺在水面上，就像躺在草地上一样，仰面朝天，充满信心，伸展四肢。如果你不害怕，水就会托住你，就像现在大地托住你一样。" 深色的草在我背脊的重量下分开，我任自己沉落，消失在井中——那圈圈转动的水之眼，沉向深处。四肢如照着图祖所教那般张开，我凝视天空。但此刻黑色的井壁正朝天空合拢，扼住它，天空成了一个遥远旋转的圆盘。我的头也在旋转……一声轰鸣……雷霆般的巨响……那水，被轰鸣震聋，将我吸入……我惊慌失措，拼命挣扎，但就在我下沉之际，一只手揪住我的头发。疼痛的冲击令我发出一声短促尖锐的叫喊，听来却似笑声。而我想笑。是的，就算笑不出声，我也真的想笑。她们害怕雷电击中熔岩壁、在井中回荡是有道理的。米莫是对的：这口井召唤了我，如今它留住了我。但最终，笑声战胜一切——即便不是我的笑声，也是众神的笑声，在雷霆般震耳欲聋的轰鸣中炸开，熔岩壁崩塌、轰然作响，携我一同坠落，又让我滑脱而去。哦，那美妙的淤泥！向下，一路向下，我滑行着。即便童年时在基亚纳的梦中，图祖将我从干草垛顶抛下，我亦从未如此滑行过。沿着熔岩壁，雨水融于那淤泥之中，使之滑腻，经数个世纪的耐心精细打磨，比音乐室打过蜡的地板还要光滑——我滑下去了，甚至无需鼓起那般我长久以来焦灼寻求的勇气。
