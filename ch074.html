Prando brakes, pulling over to the shoulder of the drive, which is flooded with brightness; the rapid banging of doors is followed by the metallic shriek of a siren. The door too is wide open, as if for a party. In the empty parlour, five figures wander about, spread here and there. Only Mela and Bambù hug each other tight, huddled on the red velvet sofa.
