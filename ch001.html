PART ONE

Chapter 1西西里乡村与童年

背景

1900 年前后的西西里：贫困、佃农关系与父权家庭塑造了一个孩子可拥有的选择。

英文原文

1 I’m four or five years old, in a muddy place, dragging a huge piece of wood. There are no trees or houses around. Only me, sweating, as I struggle to drag that rough log, my palms burning, scraped raw by the wood. I sink into the mud up to my ankles but I have to keep tugging. I don’t know why, but I have to. Let’s leave this early memory of mine just as it is: I don’t want to correct or invent things. I want to tell you how it was without changing anything. So, I was dragging that piece of wood. And after hiding it or leaving it behind, I entered a large opening in the wall, closed off only by a black curtain swarming with flies. Now I’m in the dark room where we slept and where we ate bread and olives, bread and onions. We cooked only on Sundays. My mother is sewing in a corner, her eyes wide in silence. She never speaks, my mother. She either shouts or keeps quiet. Her heavy fall of black hair is matted with flies. My sister, sitting on the ground, stares at her from two dark slits buried in folds of fat. All her life, at least as long as their lives lasted, my sister tracked her constantly, staring at her that way. And if my mother went out – which happened rarely – she had to lock her in the toilet, because my sister wouldn’t hear of being separated from her. Locked in that little room my sister would scream, tear her hair and bang her head against the wall until my mother came back, took her in her arms and silently stroked her. For years I’d heard her scream like that without paying any attention to it, until one day, tired of dragging that wood, lying on the ground and hearing her scream, I felt a kind of sweetness spreading through my body. A sweetness that then became shivers of pleasure, so that little by little, I began to hope each day that my mother would go out so that I could listen, ear pressed to the toilet door, and take pleasure from those screams. When it happened, I would close my eyes and imagine that my sister was tearing her flesh, harming herself. And so, touching myself in the spot where pee-pee comes out, my hands urged on by the screams, I discovered a pleasure greater than that of eating freshly baked bread, or fruit. My mother said that my sister Tina – ‘the cross that God justly sent us because of your father’s evil ways’ – was twenty years old. But she was only as tall as me, and so fat that, if you could remove her head, she would look like the trunk that Nonno always kept locked. Nonno, who had been a seaman, was ‘even more wicked than his son’. I had no idea what a seaman did. Tuzzu said they were men who lived on ships and went to sea … but what was the sea? Tina looked just like Nonno’s trunk, and when I was bored I would close my eyes and lop off her head. Since she was twenty years old and female, all females at twenty must surely become like her, or like my mother. For males it was different: Tuzzu was tall and had no missing teeth like Tina; his were strong and white, like the summer sky when you get up early to bake bread. His father was like him too: vigorous, with teeth that shone like Tuzzu’s when he laughed. He was always laughing, Tuzzu’s father. Our mother never laughed, and this too must have been because she was a female. But even though she never laughed and had no teeth, I hoped to turn out like her; at least she was tall and her eyes were large and gentle, and she had black hair. Tina didn’t even have hair: just a few thin strands that Mama combed out trying to cover the top of that egg. The screams have stopped. Mama must be back, hushing Tina by stroking her head. Who knows if Mama too has discovered how much pleasure you can feel by touching yourself in that spot? And Tuzzu, I wonder if Tuzzu knows? He must be harvesting reeds. The sun is high. I have to go look for him and ask him about this touching, and about the sea too. Will he still be there?
1 我四五岁光景，在一片泥泞地上，拖着一根巨大的木头。四周没有树，也没有房屋。只有我，汗流浃背，费力地拖着那根粗糙的木桩，掌心被木头磨得火辣辣地疼。泥浆没到脚踝，但我必须继续拖。我不知道为什么，可我就是必须这样做。就让我这段早年的记忆保持原样吧：我不想修正什么，也不想编造什么。我只想把那时的情形原原本本地告诉你。 于是，我拖着那根木头。把它藏好或丢下之后，我走进墙上一个很大的洞口，洞口只挂着一幅黑帘子，上面爬满了苍蝇。此刻我置身于那间黑暗的屋子，我们在那里睡觉，也在那里吃面包和橄榄，面包和洋葱。我们只有星期天才生火做饭。母亲在角落里缝补，沉默中睁大了双眼。她从不说话，我的母亲。她要么大嚷大叫，要么一声不吭。她那一头浓密的黑发上沾满了苍蝇。姐姐坐在地上，从埋在层层肥肉里的两条黑缝中死死盯着她。姐姐一生——至少是她们俩还活着的时候——就这样一直盯着母亲。而母亲要是出门——这很少发生——就得把姐姐锁在厕所里，因为姐姐绝不肯离开母亲半步。被锁在那间小屋里，姐姐便尖叫、扯头发、用头撞墙，直到母亲回来，把她抱在怀中，默默地抚摸她。 多年来，我听着她这样尖叫，从未留意，直到有一天，拖木头拖得累了，躺在地上听着她的叫声，我感到一种甜美的感觉传遍全身。那种甜美而后化作一阵阵欢愉的颤栗，于是渐渐地，我开始每天都盼着母亲出门，好把耳朵贴在厕所门上，从那些尖叫声中获取快感。 每当这时，我便闭上眼睛，想象姐姐正在撕扯自己的皮肉，伤害自己。于是，在尖叫声的催促下，我的双手抚摸着尿尿的那个地方，发现了一种比吃新鲜出炉的面包或水果更为强烈的快感。 母亲说，我姐姐蒂娜——是你父亲造的孽，上帝公正地降给我们的十字架——已经二十岁了。但她只有我这么高，而且胖得如果把头去掉，就像爷爷一直锁着的那只箱子。爷爷当过水手，比他儿子还要坏。我不知道水手是做什么的。图祖说水手是住在船上、到海上去的人……可是，海又是什么？ 蒂娜确实像爷爷的那只箱子，我无聊的时候便闭上眼睛，把她的头砍掉。既然她二十岁而且是女的，那么所有女人到了二十岁一定都变成她那副模样，或者像我母亲那样。男人不一样：图祖很高，不像蒂娜那样缺了牙齿；他的牙又结实又白，像夏天早起烤面包时的天空。他父亲也像他一样：健壮，笑起来牙像图祖的牙那样闪闪发亮。图祖的父亲总是在笑。我们的母亲从不笑，这也一定因为她是女人。但尽管她从不笑，也没有牙，我却希望自己长得像她；至少她个子高，眼睛又大又温柔，还有一头黑发。蒂娜连头发都没有：只有稀稀拉拉几根，妈妈梳理着，想盖住那颗鸡蛋一样的脑袋顶。 尖叫声停了。妈妈一定回来了，正抚摸着蒂娜的头让她安静下来。谁知道妈妈是否也发现了抚摸那个地方能带来多大的快感呢？还有图祖，不知道图祖知不知道？他一定在割芦苇。 太阳很高了。我得去找他，问他关于抚摸的事，也问问海的事。他还会在那儿吗？
