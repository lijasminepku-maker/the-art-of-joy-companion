PART THREE

Chapter 61法西斯时期与新的思想

背景

1922 年后法西斯逐步建立一党统治。自由主义、社会主义、无政府主义与共产主义并存。

英文原文

61 The light of dawn, which I had groped for in the dark, arrived to polish the shapes of the furniture and Uncle Jacopo’s books which, no longer imprisoned by glass and grillework, peacefully retold their stories. Since he had returned among us, I too had begun to refer to him as uncle, to distinguish between the troubled face that stared back at me from the photograph and the little boy with the pensive gaze of an adult. But the worm kept gnawing the bony vaults of my cranial chamber, making me get in and out of bed, open and close drawers and windows. It was stifling in that room, but when I opened the shutters I was met by a dense chill, without a cloud in the sky, the same unbroken, bright sky of summer in the dead of winter. How I had yearned for that gleaming brilliance during the long winters of my trips up north! No, I would not leave again. It was she who had to go away, taking the worm away with her. Though I lack the courage to admit it out loud, I know it has a name: jealousy. I said it, and for a moment this word, whose meaning had been unfamiliar to me before, stands apart from my emotions and I can see it; I can touch it like a vase, a glass, an object you can turn and look at from all sides. That’s the value of saying things: materialized by my voice, the worm is more nebulous, formless and ineffectual than all the feelings I had experienced up until that day. Moreover, something that I would never have imagined before, it was a carnal sensation, a constant dull ache like a tingling, a toothache … That Turk Nazim, a proletarian hero despite his nobility, living in prison and in poverty despite his wealthy, influential childhood in a villa on the Bosphorus with his little friend Joyce, daughter of an Italian ambassador and a Turkish noblewoman...
61 黎明之光，我在黑暗中摸索已久的那道光芒，终于降临，拂拭着家具的轮廓与雅各布叔叔的藏书——那些书卷，不再被玻璃与铁栅禁锢，安然地重述着它们的故事。自从他回到我们中间，我也开始称他为叔叔，以此将照片中那凝视着我的不安面容，与那个有着成人般沉思眼神的小男孩区分开来。但那蠕虫仍在啃噬我颅骨穹窿，驱使我辗转反侧，开合抽屉与窗户。房间里闷热难耐，可当我推开百叶窗，迎面而来的却是浓重的寒气，万里无云——盛夏与隆冬之间，竟是同样朗澈如洗的晴空。在北国旅居的那些漫漫冬日里，我曾何等渴念这片灿烂光芒！不，我不会再离开了。该走的是她，连同那条蠕虫一并带走。虽然我没有勇气大声承认，但我知道它有一个名字：嫉妒。我说出来了，这个词——它的含义此前对我如此陌生——瞬间与我的情感分离开来，我能看见它了；我能触摸它，如触摸一只花瓶、一只玻璃杯，一件可以翻转、从四面八方端详的物件。这便是言语之物的价值：经我之声物质化后，那条蠕虫比我此前所经历的一切情感都更加朦胧、无形而无力。更有甚者，有一种我此前绝未想象到的感受——那是一种肉体的知觉，一种持续钝痛，如刺痛，如牙痛……那个土耳其人纳齐姆，虽出身贵族，却是无产阶级的英雄；尽管童年在博斯普鲁斯海峡畔的别墅中富裕显赫地成长，与他的小伙伴乔伊斯——一位意大利大使与土耳其贵族女子之女——相伴，却过着狱中贫困的生活……
