PART ONE

Chapter 18修道院、教育与规训

背景

convent 不只是宗教场所，也是教育、慈善和纪律机构。理解称谓背后的等级，比记住全部宗教词更重要。

英文原文

18 I woke up only when my bowels, which had become a hard knot in the prior twenty-four hours, began turning into countless burning tentacles. My tongue – at first I didn't know I had a tongue – was so swollen and parched that the nursing sister had difficulty getting me to swallow a teaspoonful of a warm, fragrant liquid. 'Poor child! How she's suffering! Look at the state she's in! Three days without eating or drinking! And still she's spitting out this little bit of broth!' I wasn't the one spitting it out; on the contrary, I liked it. It was my tongue, which no longer obeyed me. Had I perhaps taken too many of those pills? I'll explain: in order to be able to sleep for so long, every night and every morning during those three days I had swallowed some of those capsules that make you sleep. The doctor had introduced me to them a long time ago. Veronal, they were called, and every night he would give me one to quiet me. At that time, despite my fear, I had never swallowed them; instead I had hidden them away for a time when I might perhaps need them. And it was a good thing, because they spared me a final meeting with Mother Leonora, and, from what I now learned, her funeral as well. The pills had proved useful, but I was so afraid of having taken too many – the doctor had told me that they could be toxic – that I couldn't help asking, 'Am I dying?' 'No, daughter, no, don't say that word again. You've done nothing but repeat that word these past three days. No, the doctor examined you. There's nothing wrong with you. Just grief and malnutrition, that's what he said, and that all we could do was hope you might regain the will to live. I see that this will has returned, since you are afraid of dying. Eat, child, and pray. Wanting to die is a terrible sin. Mother Leonora would be saddened. Think of her, and take heart. What a pity you didn't see her! Her body was all shattered, but her face was untouched, beautiful and serene. The face of a saint.' If a doctor – I wonder who this new doctor was? – had said there was nothing wrong with me, I had nothing to worry about, so I gulped down that good broth that flowed into my stomach like liquid sunshine. 'Good girl, Modesta, brava ! That's the way to make Mother Leonora happy, not by wanting to die, as you've done these past days! That's how Mother Leonora wanted to see you. Eat, eat. Don't disappoint her now that she's dead, just as you would not have disappointed her when she was alive.' So as not to disappoint Mother Leonora, I ate so much that in a few days I was back on my feet and able to listen to Sister Costanza's cackling voice without much fear of the suitcase – it was an obsession with her – that she laid on my bed when she came in. 'Gather your things, Modesta. You may take with you your precious rosary, the picture of Saint Agatha and the books that Mother Leonora in her immense generosity gave you, your personal undergarments and the bands. Don't forget the bands: continue binding your chest even after you are exposed to all the worldly dangers where you are going.' I didn't dare ask for an explanation, or take my eyes off the suitcase where already some small bedbugs evoked by Sister Costanza's words began to dot the tan-coloured leather with black. 'I am not permitted to utter worldly names and places; we no longer belong to that world. But you must not worry, because Mother Leonora has thought of you. In her magnanimity she wanted you to be the one to choose whether to join the ranks of the Lord or remain out in the world. And so that you might make this choice freely, in full awareness, she also decided that you should first come to learn about the world. That is all. They will come for you in the afternoon … I see your bewilderment, my daughter. Like you, I disagree with this, because the Lord sent you here when you were nothing but a defiled, terrified little creature and your place is here with us. But that is what her will states and that is what must be done. Go in peace. My heart is untroubled: I know that we shall meet again.' I was bewildered by the unfamiliar quality that shone through every word Sister Costanza spoke and by the gentleness her voice now held. I decided to look at her, and I nearly fainted for real. She was almost beautiful. It was as if something had straightened her up, and her lips were smiling as her eyes wandered vaguely around the room. She was dreaming about Mother Leonora's chapel stall; it was the rigid back of that oak seat that had straightened her. I almost regretted being the one who had brought about that happiness of hers. But there was no time for regret. I had to pack quickly. Troubled by that unfamiliar presence, I began gathering my things … ' Don't forget the bands, continue binding your chest … dangers … where you are going … ' That where you are going made my hands shaky and I kept dropping things. I couldn't find anything. The binding strips slipped out of my fingers and rolled into the corners, between the feet of the bed, and I had to start all over again. The suitcase was too small; it wouldn't close. Sweating, I finally managed to close it by kneeling heavily on the lid. And whether it was the exertion or Sister Costanza's radiant face that made me really angry, I sat on the suitcase and started to cry, appealing to Mother Leonora to at least tell me where she was sending me. Did she mean to take revenge?
18 我醒来时，只在过去二十四小时中凝结成硬结的肠子开始化作无数灼烧的触须。我的舌头——起初我不知自己还有舌头——肿得又大又干，护理修女费了好大劲才让我吞下一茶匙温热芬芳的液体。 "可怜的孩子！她受多大的苦啊！看看她的样子！三天不吃不喝！连这点肉汤也吐出来！" 吐出来的不是我；相反，我喜欢那汤。是我的舌头，它已不听使唤。莫非我服了太多那药丸？容我解释：为了能睡那么久，在那三天里，我每个夜晚和每个清晨都吞服了一些能让人入睡的胶囊。医生很早以前就让我认识了它们。它们叫维罗纳，每晚他都会给我一粒来安神。那时，尽管害怕，我却从未吞服过它们；相反，我将它们藏了起来，留待或许需要的时刻。这是件好事，因为它们让我免于与莱奥诺拉院长最后一面，且据我如今所知，也免于她的葬礼。药丸派上了用场，但我如此害怕服用了过量——医生曾告诉我它们可能有毒——我忍不住问道："我是不是快死了？" "不，女儿，不，别再提那个词了。这三天来你什么也没做，只是重复那个词。不，医生给你检查过了。你没什么大碍。只是悲伤与营养不良，他是这么说的，我们只能希望你能重获活下去的意志。我看这意志已经回来了，既然你害怕死亡。吃吧，孩子，祈祷吧。想死是可怕的罪过。莱奥诺拉院长会伤心的。想想她，振作起来。真可惜你没见到她！她的身体粉碎了，但面容完好无损，美丽而安详。圣徒的面容。" 若医生说——我好奇这位新医生是谁？——我没有什么大碍，我便无须担心，于是我狼吞虎咽地喝下那碗美味的肉汤，它如液态阳光般流入我的胃中。 "好姑娘，莫黛斯塔，好样的！这才是让莱奥诺拉院长高兴的方式，而非如你这几日所做的那样想死！这才是莱奥诺拉院长想看到你的样子。吃吧，吃吧。别让她失望，如今她已死，正如她活着时你不会令她失望一般。" 为了不让莱奥诺拉院长失望，我吃了那么多，几天后我便重新站了起来，能够听科斯坦扎修女那嘎嘎的声音，而不对那只手提箱——那是她的执念——感到太多恐惧，她进来时将箱子放在我床上。 "收拾你的东西，莫黛斯塔。你可以带上你珍贵的念珠、圣阿加莎的画像，以及莱奥诺拉院长凭她无比慷慨赠予你的书籍，你的个人内衣和束带。别忘了束带：即便你暴露于你去往之地的所有世俗危险中，也要继续束住胸口。" 我不敢要求解释，也不敢将目光从手提箱上移开——科斯坦扎修女的话已让一些小小的臭虫开始用黑点点缀那棕褐色的皮革。 "我不被允许说出世俗的名字与地点；我们已不再属于那个世界。但你不必担心，因为莱奥诺拉院长已为你考虑周全。她以宽广胸怀，希望由你自己选择是加入主的行列，还是留在尘世之中。为使你能在充分认知的情况下自由做出这选择，她还决定你应先去了解这个世界。仅此而已。下午会有人来接你……我看到你的困惑，我的女儿。与你一样，我不同意此事，因为主将你送到这里时，你不过是一个被玷污、受惊吓的小东西，你的位置是和我们在一起。但她的遗嘱如此声明，此事便必须照办。安心去吧。我心无忧：我知道我们必将重逢。" 我因科斯坦扎修女每句话中闪耀的陌生特质而困惑，也因她声音此刻所怀的温柔而迷惑。我决定看看她，结果我几乎真的晕了过去。她几乎称得上美丽。仿佛有什么东西将她挺直了，她的嘴唇微笑着，眼睛茫然地环视房间。她正梦想着莱奥诺拉院长的小堂座位；是那橡木座椅坚硬的靠背将她挺直了。我几乎后悔自己成了给她带来那份幸福的人。但没有时间后悔。我得赶紧收拾。 被那不熟悉的在场搅得心烦意乱，我开始收拾东西……"别忘了束带，继续束住胸口……危险……你去往之地……"那个"你去往之地"让我双手颤抖，东西不断掉落。我什么也找不到。束胸带从指间滑落，滚到角落里、床脚之间，我不得不从头再来。手提箱太小了，盖不上。汗流浃背，我终于重重地跪在箱盖上，将它合上。不知是用力过度还是科斯坦扎修女容光焕发的脸让我真的生气了，我坐在手提箱上哭了起来，向莱奥诺拉院长恳求，至少告诉我她要把我送去哪里。她是要报复吗？
